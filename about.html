About

Hi, I'm Radhika.

A product designer with a soft spot for the small, human moments in an interface. I believe good design should feel like a friendly nook — not a sterile dashboard.

Product designDesign systems PrototypingIllustration User researchFigma

I like to start with research and sit with a problem before touching a single frame — understanding what's actually breaking and where the gaps are.

I love capturing stills — be it people, places, or just a quiet moment.

I adore colour, and I secretly judge people who only ever wear black.

Off-screen I'm either travelling, hoarding stationery, or re-reading the same comfort books for the hundredth time.

I'm a certified water baby and emotionally stable only within 500 metres of a large body of water.

Bits, bops and behind the screen

A tiny note of things I love

Sticky notes that pile up faster than I can use them. The first sip of coffee before the day fully starts. Tiny doodles in the margins. The hush right before a good idea lands.

Mostly, I just like making small, warm things — and leaving a little personality behind in everything I ship.
