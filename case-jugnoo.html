Jugnoo — Vehicle Branding

A system that verifies vehicle branding daily through AI and rewards consistency through a series of incentive structure, designed to balance business compliance needs with driver trust and retention.

  • Product design
  • Mobile app
  • UX research
  • Case study
My roleProduct Designer
Timeline6 weeks
The problem
Verify auto-rickshaw branding every day without losing driver trust.
What I did
AI video verification, a ₹10 daily payout, a ₹100 reward for every 30-day streak, and fair edge cases (2 attempts a day, 1 skip per 15 days).
Takeaway
Incentives are part of the experience — the numbers deserve as much design as the screens.
Impact it created
Daily manual spot-checks were replaced by an AI video check, and drivers got a transparent, paid reason to keep branding up: ₹10 for every approved day plus ₹100 for every 30-day streak.

Jugnoo is a ride-hailing platform operating across India. As part of its marketing, it pays auto drivers to display Jugnoo posters on their vehicles, turning every rickshaw into a moving ad at a fraction of the cost of traditional advertising. The hard part was proving, every single day, that the posters were still up, without making drivers feel policed.
