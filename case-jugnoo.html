Jugnoo - Vehicle Branding

A system that verifies vehicle branding daily through AI and rewards consistency through a series of incentive structure, designed to balance business compliance needs with driver trust and retention.

My roleProduct Designer
Timeline6 weeks
TeamJungleworks · Jugnoo
ToolsFigma

The Problem

Verify auto-rickshaw branding every day without losing driver trust.

What I Did

AI video verification, a ₹10 daily payout, a ₹100 reward for every 30-day streak, and fair edge cases (2 attempts a day, 1 skip per 15 days).

Takeaway

Incentives are part of the experience - the numbers deserve as much design as the screens.

Impact

Improved driver retention and consistent branding compliance across the fleet.

Turning every rickshaw into a moving ad.

Jugnoo is a ride-hailing platform operating across India, connecting everyday commuters with rides.

As part of its marketing, Jugnoo pays Auto drivers to display Jugnoo posters on their vehicles, turning every rickshaw into a moving ad at a fraction of the cost of traditional advertising.

Design approach

The branding verification system evolved through phases, solving specific problems at each stage.

Rather than trying to design a complete solution upfront, each phase revealed what drivers actually needed and what motivated them to participate consistently.

Key design goals

01Fairness

Rules designed around realistic driver scenarios - vehicle downtime, emergencies, rejected videos. The system penalizes non-compliance, not bad luck.

02Consistency

Daily rewards and streak incentives work together to keep both short-term and long-term motivation aligned.

03Simplicity

Verification, rewards, and history kept in one clear flow. Drivers understand their status without checking multiple screens.

Designing the incentive system

The solution combined AI verification with a thoughtful incentive structure: ₹10 daily for verified branding, plus ₹100 for every 30-day consistency streak. Fair edge cases (2 attempts per day, 1 skip per 15 days) built trust without punishing bad luck.

Key learnings

01Incentives are part of experience

The ₹10 daily reward, 30-day streak, and ₹100 bonus directly affect driver response. These numbers needed as much design thought as the screens.

02Fair rules build trust

Drivers needed to believe the system was fair. Allowing retries and occasional skips proved more effective than strict enforcement.
