The project

Growth Score, and the questions ratings can’t ask.

Connect is a feature within Growth Score that lets businesses create personalised feedback forms and distribute them through email campaigns.

Growth Score is a B2B customer feedback and reputation management platform that helps businesses understand how customers feel about their products and services. Through features like NPS, CSAT, reviews, referrals, and testimonials, businesses can collect feedback, measure satisfaction, and build stronger customer relationships.

But customer feedback isn't always about ratings. Sometimes, businesses need to ask specific questions to understand what their customers really want. Teams can customise their questions, apply scoring, and collect responses in one place, making it easier to turn feedback into insight.

Here is what this project taught me

01Balance Business Goals with User Needs

This project taught me to think from both a business and a user perspective. I learned how to simplify complex processes without limiting flexibility, while building a product structure that can scale and adapt to future features. Thinking beyond the immediate interface helped me understand how design decisions can support both business goals and user needs.

02Make Complex Workflows Easier to Navigate

I learned that showing too many options on one screen can overwhelm users and increase decision-making effort. Breaking a process into smaller, guided steps makes it easier to follow. I also realized how important editing, deleting, and going back are when designing forms, as users need the freedom to correct mistakes without starting over.
