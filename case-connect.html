Feedback that asks the right questions.

Growth Score is a B2B feedback and reputation platform - NPS, CSAT, reviews, referrals and testimonials in one place.

But feedback isn't always a rating. Sometimes a business needs to ask its own specific questions, and a star score can't answer those. We needed a way for businesses to create custom feedback forms, collect responses, and see scoring all in one place.

Designing the feedback form builder

The solution combined reusable templates with flexible customization, email campaigns for distribution, and scoring logic to help businesses get deeper insights from their customers.
