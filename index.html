Why me

What your team gets on day one

01Simpler complex flows

Breaks multi-step processes into guided, reversible steps — like the Connect form builder, where users can edit, delete and go back without starting over.

02Fairness & trust by design

Rules built around real scenarios, not ideal ones — at Jugnoo a genuine miss doesn’t feel like a punishment, so drivers stay.

03Consistency at scale

Led UX across On-Demand, Driver, Carpool and Car Rental — five live products that feel like one ecosystem.

04Faster exploration, with AI

AI-assisted workflows for exploration and iteration, with human judgement kept on the decisions that matter.
