Bringing the film to life through illustration.

A fan poster for Disney Pixar's Elemental, built around the film's central theme: "Opposites react".

The poster features water meeting fire, illustrated in Adobe Illustrator with the title lettered in Devanagari, blending the film's narrative with typography and custom illustration.

Design approach

This project was an exercise in vector illustration and lettering work. The composition plays on the contrast between water and fire elements, with careful attention to color, form, and the integration of Devanagari script. Each element was drawn and refined in Adobe Illustrator to create a cohesive, poster-worthy composition.
