About the project

A healthier, happier you — in one hub.

Through this project I wanted to solve a real life problem of taking care of our health in today's fast-paced world.

Managing our health often takes a backseat. Wellspring was born from the desire to tackle this challenge. This project aims to empower individuals by providing a central hub for all their healthcare needs. By simplifying tasks like tracking symptoms, scheduling appointments, and managing medications, Wellspring hopes to make proactive health management more accessible and convenient, ultimately leading to a healthier and happier you.

Key takeaways

What I took away

1User needs over Aesthetics

I used to think design was all about aesthetics, but this project showed me the importance of prioritizing the user. By understanding their needs, frustrations, and goals, I wanted to create a design that works for them.

2The power of iteration

This project taught me the power of iteration. I constantly tested my ideas with users, gathered feedback, and then refined my designs based on their input. It's a cycle of learning and improvement that leads to a much stronger final product.

3Less is more

I understood the concept of Less is more and the importance of clear and concise design. Simplicity is the way to go, by focusing on intuitive navigation, easy-to-understand visuals, and user-friendly interactions.

As a student, I'm constantly eager to learn and grow, and this project opened my eyes to the fascinating world of user experience design. There's so much more to discover, and I can't wait to put these learnings into practice on future projects!
