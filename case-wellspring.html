Making health management proactive instead of reactive.

In a fast-paced life, looking after your own health is the first thing to slip.

Wellspring started from that: a single place to track symptoms, book appointments and stay on top of medication, so managing your health feels proactive instead of reactive.

Discovery through testing

This project took me through the complete design process - from understanding user needs through empathy mapping and user personas, to ideation, prototyping, and usability testing.

Designing the healthcare hub

The solution focused on four core areas: symptom tracking, appointment scheduling, medication management, and health history. Each feature was designed around actual user needs discovered during research.

Key learnings

01User needs over aesthetics

Early iterations focused on visual polish, but user testing revealed that clarity and functionality mattered far more. Simplified designs performed better in testing.

02Iterate continuously

Each round of testing uncovered new insights about how users approached health management. Rapid iteration based on feedback shaped the final design.

03Less is more

Removing unnecessary features and information made the app feel less overwhelming. Users appreciated the focused approach to health management.
