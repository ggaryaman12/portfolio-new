Wellspring — A Healthcare UI/UX project

Manage your health effortlessly — track symptoms, make appointments, take meds, and record it all in one place.

  • Mobile app
  • Healthcare
  • UI / UX
  • Figma
My roleUI/UX Designer
TimelineCase study
The problem
Managing your health takes a backseat in a fast-paced life.
What I did
A single hub to track symptoms, schedule appointments and manage medications — taken from discovery through to testing.
Takeaway
User needs over aesthetics. Iterate. Less is more.
Impact it created
A self-directed project carried through the full process — discovery, empathy, definition, ideation, prototyping and usability testing — and the work that shifted how I prioritise user needs over visual polish.

In a fast-paced life, looking after your own health is the first thing to slip. Wellspring started from that: a single place to track symptoms, book appointments and stay on top of medication, so managing your health feels proactive instead of reactive.
